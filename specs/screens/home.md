# Home Screen Spec

## Purpose

Present the available apps and provide a clear path to each one.

## Expected Behavior

- The page stays intentionally restrained: one short title followed by the app cards.
- It avoids promotional statements about the collection or its creator.
- Available apps appear as accessible cards with icon, name, short promise, and link.
- V1 contains only Goalzy, while the layout remains natural with one or many apps.
- Header navigation exposes the current language and its alternative.

## Edge Cases

- A single app card must not look like an empty grid.
- Long translated copy must wrap without clipping or horizontal scrolling.

## V1 Non-Goals

- Filtering, search, accounts, newsletters, and release feeds.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Goalzy is immediately identifiable and reachable.
- The page works in French and English at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Keyboard and screen-reader users can understand and activate the app card.
